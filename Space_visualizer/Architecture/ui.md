# ui (`src/ui`)

Back to [[Home]] · [[Overview]] · Built in [[2026-10-07-m2a3-interface-and-tour]] (spec §4)

**Layout:** header top-left, control panel top-right, pill labels over the scene, bottom bar along the bottom, facts drawer on the right. Dark frosted-glass panels with hairline borders (tokens in `src/styles`), Inter for text and a monospace face for numbers, cyan for interaction, amber for warnings and each body's accent colour (`body.display.color`) for its headline and values. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components
- **Header** (`role="banner"`, named "Selected body" when a body is selected). Overview mode: "Solar System", the UTC date, scale and bodies in view (`sceneStatsStore`, projected at about 2 Hz). Selected mode: kind and distance, the name in the body's accent colour, cited chips (From Earth or From the Sun, light time, day length, mean temperature), a narrative (once the details load, a cited sentence about the first major atmosphere gas and the magnetic field replaces the day-and-temperature sentence, which the chips already show; never a number without its source), and the buttons Facts & sources, Centre view (Orbit view only) and Deselect (×). Each chip carries `[n]`, a link that opens the drawer at that source. On phones it is compact (shorter chips, "Show more" for the narrative).
- **Narrative** (`narrative.ts`): two or three sentences built only from cited facts and live values. A sentence whose fact is missing is dropped; every number is a `value` part with its source id. Stars get their own sentence (spectral type and radius). A body with a parent that is not a star reads "orbits <parent> every", not "circles the Sun".
- **`citationModel`** (`citations.ts`): the single source numbering shared by the header and the drawer: the body's own sources in display order, then the live position model if it is not already listed, then the body's details sources (M2b.1, in `detailsSourceOrder`; since M2b.3 it ends with the interior radius and each `gradual` sentence's source, so the depth ruler and the layer card always have a number for them). Details only ever append, so a number shown before the details load never changes after they load. Header `[n]` and drawer list items always agree.
- **`liveStats`** (`liveStats.ts`): distances from the Sun and Earth and light time (exact SI speed of light) with the source that backs them. `format.ts` holds the number formatters.
- **ControlPanel:** View (Orbit, Follow), Style (Realistic; Exhibit is disabled until M2c), Scale (Readable, True scale), Quality (Auto, High, Medium, Low) and the Camera effects checkbox, plus a key hint. It publishes `--control-panel-bottom`. On phones it collapses behind a "Controls" button and opens as a dropdown that is topmost and opaque.
- **FactsDrawer** (`role="complementary"` named "Body information"): description, physical and orbital facts, live values, accuracy lines, Imagery (every layer cited; procedural visuals labelled illustrative; the Venus radar toggle; the notes follow what is on screen, and the header carries a "Radar view · not visible light" tag while the radar shows) and the numbered sources. When closed it is `aria-hidden` and `inert`. Opening it from a header chip scrolls to and highlights that source. On phones it is a bottom sheet between the header and the bottom bar.
- **DetailsSections** (`DetailsSections.tsx`, M2b.1): drawn in the drawer between Position accuracy and Imagery. Shows "Loading details…" while the store loads, "Details unavailable" with a Retry button on `missing` or `error` (the rest of the drawer, including Sources, still works), and otherwise the sections that have data, each with `id="details-<section>"` and cited with the shared `.cite` links: Interior (each layer reads outside in, "from <outer bound> to <inner bound>", every bound in the form its source states it: km deep, radius in km, % of the radius, the surface or the centre; the state, cited when a source states it, and an uncertain tag), Atmosphere (major gases by volume, minor gases in ppm, a note), Magnetic field (a dipole table, "No global magnetic field", or a note), Key missions (agency, type, outcome, dates; launch order), Discovery ("Discovered by … on <date>") and Named features (the top 10 by diameter with the Gazetteer total and cut-off). A null part renders no section.
- **BottomBar:** the UTC date, Reverse, Play/Pause, Speed, Now, "Go to date" (`YYYY-MM-DD[ HH:MM[:SS]]`, years -4712 to 9999, impossible dates rejected), the Tour button ("Tour" / "Stop tour", `aria-pressed`), the 1800-2050 timeline scrubber (`timeline.ts`), a validity status (amber outside 1800-2050, an extrapolation warning outside 3000 BC-AD 3000) and the sky credit. It publishes `--bottom-bar-height`. The compact phone bar is about 157-170 px.
- **Pill labels** (`src/scene/BodyLabel.tsx`): a dot, the NAME in capitals and a datum (distance, or light time for the selected body), updated at 4 Hz. Each label's accessible name is the body name. `labelLayout.ts` has `resolveOverlaps` (hide the lower priority of two overlapping boxes, 4 px padding) and `labelPriority` (selected, then hovered, then larger pixel radius, ties alphabetical). `useLabelDeclutter` reads label rectangles at 10 Hz and applies it. In the overview this hides Earth behind the Sun's label; hovering or selecting brings a label back.
- **Status screens:** loading, data error with "Try again", and "3D graphics are not available" when WebGL is missing.

## State and keys
- **`uiStore`:** `drawerOpen`, `controlsOpen` (phone), `focusSource`/`revealSource`. The drawer and the phone Controls dropdown are mutually exclusive: opening one closes the other.
- **`tourStore`:** `active`, `seq`, `start`, `stop`, `toggle`. See Tour in [[camera]].
- **`hotkeys.ts`** is the single owner of keys: **1** Orbit, **2** Follow, **Esc** Orbit, **T** Tour, **Q** cycles Quality, **E** toggles Camera effects, **F** toggles the drawer, **X** enters Explore (inside Explore the keys change: see "Explore (M2b.3)" below). Keys are ignored while typing (text-entry inputs, select, textarea, contentEditable; a focused range slider, checkbox or button does not count), with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held, and on auto-repeat. **F** does nothing without a selection. While a tour runs any other key stops it (a bare modifier does not); Space and Enter on the Tour button leave stopping to the button's own click. `useTourExit` stops the tour on pointer, wheel and touch input, except on the Tour button, which toggles. Header and drawer live values update at about 4 Hz (`useThrottledJd`).

## Published layout variables
CSS custom properties on the document root, so panels never overlap: `--control-panel-bottom` (ControlPanel), `--bottom-bar-height` (BottomBar: distance from the viewport bottom to the bar's top), `--header-bottom` (Header). The drawer's top and bottom, and the phone header and Controls limits, are computed from them (with fallbacks before they are published).

**Breakpoints:** at 1140 px and narrower the control panel collapses behind its Controls button (the 520 px header and the 560 px panel would otherwise meet); at 940 px and narrower the 380 px drawer sits under the header (`top` from `--header-bottom`); at 720 px and narrower the phone layout applies (compact header and bar, the drawer as a bottom sheet). The open Controls dropdown may overlay the header; it is topmost and opaque. Layout tests (`e2e/layout.spec.ts`) check that the header, control panel, bottom bar and drawer (open and closed) do not intersect, and that the page does not scroll horizontally, at 390x844, 800x1000, 1024x768, 1100x800 and 1280x720.

## Look and accessibility
Stable names: the drawer is "Body information"; controls keep their visible names (Orbit, Follow, Readable, True scale, Auto, High, Medium, Low, Camera effects, Now, Go, Tour). Slide and fade transitions are switched off under reduced motion (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` in `app.css`), the drawer scroll to a source uses `auto` instead of `smooth`, and Tour flights become cuts.

## The Moon (M2b.2)
- **Header:** for a satellite the subtitle reads "Moon of Earth · 384,400 km" (category, parent, `formatKm` of the live distance from the parent). The chips are From Earth, **Phase** (for example "50% · First quarter"), Light time, Day length. Phase comes from `moonPhase` through `liveStats` and exists only for satellites of Earth.
- **Distances:** `formatKm` (in `format.ts`) rounds first and then picks the format, so a value never reads "1000.0 km". The body label and the narrative use it for satellites. The narrative says "The Moon orbits Earth every 27.3 days at an average 384,400 km" and no longer "0.00 AU". The article is added for the Moon by name; other moons take none.
- **Live source:** `liveStats.sourceId` is the model behind the value at that date. For a satellite outside its table it is the mean elements (`jpl-sats-elem`), not Horizons. The citation numbering does not change when the date crosses the table edge.
- **Accuracy lines:** for a satellite the drawer shows the table figure (0.904 km, 0.4792 arcsec over 366,720 held-out samples) and, outside the table, the mean-elements figure. Earth shows its centre check.
- **Citations:** the drawer cites the barycentre sources for Earth's position. Drawer Imagery for the Moon: the SVS credit, "optimized for aesthetics, not science", and illustrative tags for relief, earthshine and the eclipse tint (`illustrativeNotes(body, { parent })`).
- **Details sections:** the atmosphere section is headed "Exosphere" for `kind: 'exosphere'`, with rows such as "Helium 4 (⁴He) 40,000 per cm³ (upper limit)".

## Explore (M2b.3)
Built in [[2026-10-30-m2b3-explore-interior]] from spec [[2026-10-30-m2b3-explore-interior-design]] (§3 layout, §5 chart). Files: `src/ui/explore/`, `src/ui/interiorText.ts`, `src/ui/AppChrome.tsx`, `src/styles/explore.css`, `src/scene/interior/InteriorLabels.tsx`.
- **Explore button:** in the selected body's header, next to Facts & sources ("Explore (X)"). It is an action, not a toggle, so it has **no `aria-pressed`** and carries `aria-keyshortcuts="X"` (a ruling that corrected the plan). It shows for every body; a body with no interior entry opens Whole only with "No interior data yet".
- **AppChrome (`AppChrome.tsx`) and the session signal:** AppChrome renders the header, control panel, facts drawer and bottom bar inside a `display: contents` wrapper. When `useExploreOwnsScreen()` turns true (Explore active, or the scene still closing the body; see [[state]]) the parts fade out over `CHROME_FADE_MS` 200 ms, inert while they fade, and are then removed; when it turns false they fade back in. Under reduced motion they go and return at once. The interface therefore returns only after the body has closed. Focus: when the chrome returns, focus goes to the header's Explore button; if it is gone (body deselected) the first focusable control in the control panel gets it. Focus is never taken from an element the user has already focused.
- **ExploreOverlay (`ExploreOverlay.tsx`):** centre-stage layout, rendered by `App.tsx` while Explore is active.
  - **Top:** kind and name ("Explore · Interior" over the body name in its accent colour), the view switch, and "Leave Explore" (✕). The switch is a `role="radiogroup"` named "Interior view" with Whole, Exploded and Half-section, a roving tabindex and arrow keys. In its place: "Loading the interior…" (a status) while the details load, "No interior data yet" when the body has no details file or no interior, and "Could not load the interior." with Retry (an alert; Retry calls `useDetailsStore.retry`) when the load failed. The switch is shown only when a view can open. On a phone the grid reflows into rows.
  - **Notes:** an "Estimate" tag when any layer is an estimate; "Layer sizes are not to scale" when every layer is illustrative (Uranus and Neptune); an "Illustrative colours" link that opens the layer card and focuses its colours note.
  - **Bottom strip:** `compositionPreview` text with `[n]` cites, two mini log bars and "+N more · Open full chart ›". With nothing to chart it shows the cited note or "No atmosphere".
  - **Chart panel:** a dialog over the strip holding `CompositionChart` (a bottom sheet on phones). It closes with ✕ or Esc. Under the chart it lists the sources its values cite (`SourcesList`, items `chart-source-<id>`). Focus goes to its ✕ on open (or to the cited source when a strip `[n]` opened it) and back to "Open full chart" on close. It has no `aria-modal` or focus trap (deferred).
  - **Focus on entry:** to the checked view radio (or ✕ with no switch). Every part the labels must avoid carries `data-explore-reserved`.
  - **Citations in Explore:** the drawer is not on screen, so the overlay sets `CiteTargetContext`: every `[n]` in the strip and the chart has a tooltip naming the source and publisher, and links to the chart panel's Sources list (the bars' sources, the chart note's and the strip's); a strip `[n]` opens the panel on its source. The layer card sets its own target (its Sources list, `source-<id>`). The `[n]` on the floating labels and the depth ruler show the same tooltip but are not links: the ruler lets clicks through to the scene, and a label's click opens its card, which lists and links the sources.
  - **Focus:** on entry focus goes to the checked view (or ✕ when there is no switch). If the focused control goes away (Retry once the data loads, the card closed by Esc), focus returns there too, never to the page body.
  - Components subscribe narrowly: nothing subscribes to `progress`, which updates every frame while a view opens.
- **Floating labels (`InteriorLabels.tsx`, `labelAnchors.ts`, `labelLayout.ts`):**
  - `InteriorLabels` (DOM, inside `.label-layer`) renders one button per layer: name plus an Estimate or Illustrative size tag, a detail line (state · bound `[n]`), and an SVG leader line with an anchor dot. `[n]` inside a label is plain text, because a link inside a button is invalid; the card carries the links.
  - `InteriorLabelProjector` runs in the Canvas at `useFrame` priority -0.3. Each frame it projects the anchors, reads the reserved rects, calls `labelLayout` and writes transforms and leaders. It reads the stores with `getState()` and makes no React updates per frame. Label sizes (full and collapsed) are measured on content or selection change, on resize and on `fonts.ready`.
  - **Anchors (`labelAnchors.ts`, pure):** sides alternate from the right, outermost first. Exploded: on the visible band of each lifted cap, between its base ring and the base of the cap above (the innermost sphere at its equator). Half-section: on the cut face at mid-ring, tilted by 25 degrees: right-side layers below the centre (the depth ruler takes the upper right), left-side layers up then down.
  - **`labelLayout` (pure):** highest priority first, the selected layer ranked first. Each label tries, in order: full on its side (shifting up to 35 % of the height), collapsed (name only) on its side, full on the other side, collapsed on the other side, then hidden. Columns sit outside the body circle when the viewport allows. An "uncross" pass keeps each side's labels in the order of their anchors, so leaders never cross. A seeded property test (600 random sets at three viewports) checks no overlaps.
  - **Depth ruler (Half-section, wider than 720 px; `depthRuler.ts`, drawn by `InteriorLabels`):** a slim scale beside the face's right edge, from the surface (0 km) at the top of the face to the centre. `depthRulerTicks` gives one tick per layer boundary at its drawn radius; each value is the cited bound as a depth below the surface: a depth bound keeps its numbers ("≈" where the wording table says "about"), a radius bound is the cited radius minus it (Earth 6,371 - 3,485 = 2,886 km), a percent bound becomes an approximate depth to three figures ("≈ 25,900 km"). An illustrative boundary has a tick and no number. The centre shows the cited radius the bounds were converted with (`interior.radiusKm`, "(centre)"). Each value carries its `[n]`; a paper bound has an Estimate tag. The projector puts each tick at the top of its drawn ring and calls `placeRuler`: crowded values are pushed apart; if the values would cross a reserved part (the card, the bars) only the ticks show; if even those would, it hides. Its rect is then reserved, so the labels keep clear of it. Hidden on a phone (the layer list carries the bounds).
  - Labels fade in over the last 40 % of the opening. They are hidden in Whole, while closing, after leave and while the chart is open.
- **Phone variant (≤ 720 px):** the labels become numbered dots at the anchors, laid out by the same function as dot-sized boxes. The overlay adds a tap-to-open numbered "Layers" list, the layer card and the chart become bottom sheets.
- **Layer card (`LayerCard.tsx`):** a right-side glass card under ✕. Content: "Layer i of n", the name, tags (state `[n]`, Estimate, Illustrative size, Uncertain, Drawn thicker than scale), the extent in the drawer's form (cited), "Made of" (cited), notes (estimate: from a peer-reviewed paper, not an official source; illustrative size; uncertain; range drawn as a soft band; "Illustrative colours: they show state and heat, not real colours") and a "Sources" list using the shared `citationModel` numbering, so it matches the header and the drawer. An illustrative layer shows no extent row (its tag says so). A range bound adds "The source gives a range, drawn as a soft band". A gradual boundary adds "No sharp edge: the source describes a gradual change into the layer above [n]. The soft edge is illustrative" on the core, and the matching "No sharp lower edge" note on the layer above. Closing: "Close layer card".
- **Wording table (`interiorText.ts`):** the drawer's bound formatter moved here so the drawer and the card share it. Short label bounds read "to 30 km deep", "to 3,485 km radius", "to 25 % of the radius"; the innermost layer shows its own extent ("1,221 km radius"). The `WORDING` table carries qualifiers that the source sentence has but the data does not: Saturn's diffuse core "about 60 % of the radius" and Venus's crust and mantle top "about 20 km deep (global average)". It quotes the source sentences in comments. It also shows in the facts drawer. This is logged as debt: move the qualifier into the data (see the carry-over note).
- **Composition strip and chart (`compositionModel.ts`, `CompositionChart.tsx`):** `compositionModel(body, details)` builds one ranked list per body on one log axis. Percent and ppm share the axis (1 % = 10,000 ppm); each value is printed in its source's own unit. The Moon's exosphere (per cm³, upper limits) has its own axis. Zero amounts are dropped. The title names the basis from the data ("Atmosphere · by volume, dry air", "Photosphere"). Ticks are one per power of ten. `compositionPreview(model, details, body?)` is the strip text; "No atmosphere" is cited only when the surface-pressure fact is 0 bar. The chart is HTML and CSS (`role="list"`, rows are focusable list items, bars are rounded CSS bars of one accent hue) with a tooltip on hover or focus and a Table toggle (`aria-pressed`) that swaps in a real table. Citation labels read "Source 5 for Nitrogen". Under 480 px, tick labels switch to 1k, 100k, 1M and every other decade is hidden. In the drawer the chart's `[n]` links point to `#source-...`; in Explore they point to the chart panel's own Sources list (see Citations in Explore).
- **Keys (`hotkeys.ts`, still the single owner):** **X** enters Explore with a selection (not while Explore is open). Inside Explore: **1 / 2 / 3** pick Whole, Exploded, Half-section; **Esc** closes the chart, else the layer card, else leaves (one level per press); **T** does nothing; **F** is blocked (the drawer is hidden); **Q** and **E** still work. Outside Explore 1, 2 and Esc keep meaning Orbit and Follow. `hotkeyAction(e, { active })` stays pure; `useHotkeys` passes the store state. The control panel's key hint lists "X explore".
