# ui (`src/ui`)

Back to [[Home]] · [[Overview]] · Built in [[2026-10-07-m2a3-interface-and-tour]] (spec §4)

**Layout:** header top-left, control panel top-right, pill labels over the scene, bottom bar along the bottom, facts drawer on the right. Dark frosted-glass panels with hairline borders (tokens in `src/styles`), Inter for text and a monospace face for numbers, cyan for interaction, amber for warnings and each body's accent colour (`body.display.color`) for its headline and values. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Components
- **Header** (`role="banner"`, named "Selected body" when a body is selected). Overview mode: "Solar System", the UTC date, scale and bodies in view (`sceneStatsStore`, projected at about 2 Hz). Selected mode: kind and distance, the name in the body's accent colour, cited chips (From Earth or From the Sun, light time, day length, mean temperature), a narrative (once the details load, a cited sentence about the first major atmosphere gas and the magnetic field replaces the day-and-temperature sentence, which the chips already show; never a number without its source), and the buttons Facts & sources, Centre view (Orbit view only) and Deselect (×). Each chip carries `[n]`, a link that opens the drawer at that source. On phones it is compact (shorter chips, "Show more" for the narrative).
- **Narrative** (`narrative.ts`): two or three sentences built only from cited facts and live values. A sentence whose fact is missing is dropped; every number is a `value` part with its source id. Stars get their own sentence (spectral type and radius). A body with a parent that is not a star reads "orbits <parent> every", not "circles the Sun".
- **`citationModel`** (`citations.ts`): the single source numbering shared by the header and the drawer: the body's own sources in display order, then the live position model if it is not already listed, then the body's details sources (M2b.1, in `detailsSourceOrder`). Details only ever append, so a number shown before the details load never changes after they load. Header `[n]` and drawer list items always agree.
- **`liveStats`** (`liveStats.ts`): distances from the Sun and Earth and light time (exact SI speed of light) with the source that backs them. `format.ts` holds the number formatters.
- **ControlPanel:** View (Orbit, Follow), Style (Realistic; Exhibit is disabled until M2c), Scale (Readable, True scale), Quality (Auto, High, Medium, Low) and the Camera effects checkbox, plus a key hint. It publishes `--control-panel-bottom`. On phones it collapses behind a "Controls" button and opens as a dropdown that is topmost and opaque.
- **FactsDrawer** (`role="complementary"` named "Body information"): description, physical and orbital facts, live values, accuracy lines, Imagery (every layer cited; procedural visuals labelled illustrative; the Venus radar toggle; the notes follow what is on screen, and the header carries a "Radar view · not visible light" tag while the radar shows) and the numbered sources. When closed it is `aria-hidden` and `inert`. Opening it from a header chip scrolls to and highlights that source. On phones it is a bottom sheet between the header and the bottom bar.
- **DetailsSections** (`DetailsSections.tsx`, M2b.1): drawn in the drawer between Position accuracy and Imagery. Shows "Loading details…" while the store loads, "Details unavailable" with a Retry button on `missing` or `error` (the rest of the drawer, including Sources, still works), and otherwise the sections that have data, each with `id="details-<section>"` and cited with the shared `.cite` links: Interior (each layer reads outside in, "from <outer bound> to <inner bound>", every bound in the form its source states it: km deep, radius in km, % of the radius, the surface or the centre; the state, cited when a source states it, and an uncertain tag), Atmosphere (major gases by volume, minor gases in ppm, a note), Magnetic field (a dipole table, "No global magnetic field", or a note), Key missions (agency, type, outcome, dates; launch order), Discovery ("Discovered by … on <date>") and Named features (the top 10 by diameter with the Gazetteer total and cut-off). A null part renders no section.
- **BottomBar:** the UTC date, Reverse, Play/Pause, Speed, Now, "Go to date" (`YYYY-MM-DD[ HH:MM[:SS]]`, years -4712 to 9999, impossible dates rejected), the Tour button ("Tour" / "Stop tour", `aria-pressed`), the 1800-2050 timeline scrubber (`timeline.ts`), a validity status (amber outside 1800-2050, an extrapolation warning outside 3000 BC-AD 3000) and the sky credit. It publishes `--bottom-bar-height`. The compact phone bar is about 157-170 px.
- **Pill labels** (`src/scene/BodyLabel.tsx`): a dot, the NAME in capitals and a datum (distance, or light time for the selected body), updated at 4 Hz. Each label's accessible name is the body name. `labelLayout.ts` has `resolveOverlaps` (hide the lower priority of two overlapping boxes, 4 px padding) and `labelPriority` (selected, then hovered, then larger pixel radius, ties alphabetical). `useLabelDeclutter` reads label rectangles at 10 Hz and applies it. In the overview this hides Earth behind the Sun's label; hovering or selecting brings a label back.
- **Status screens:** loading, data error with "Try again", and "3D graphics are not available" when WebGL is missing.

## State and keys
- **`uiStore`:** `drawerOpen`, `controlsOpen` (phone), `focusSource`/`revealSource`. The drawer and the phone Controls dropdown are mutually exclusive: opening one closes the other.
- **`tourStore`:** `active`, `seq`, `start`, `stop`, `toggle`. See Tour in [[camera]].
- **`hotkeys.ts`** is the single owner of keys: **1** Orbit, **2** Follow, **Esc** Orbit, **T** Tour, **Q** cycles Quality, **E** toggles Camera effects, **F** toggles the drawer. Keys are ignored while typing (text-entry inputs, select, textarea, contentEditable; a focused range slider, checkbox or button does not count), with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held, and on auto-repeat. **F** does nothing without a selection. While a tour runs any other key stops it (a bare modifier does not); Space and Enter on the Tour button leave stopping to the button's own click. `useTourExit` stops the tour on pointer, wheel and touch input, except on the Tour button, which toggles. Header and drawer live values update at about 4 Hz (`useThrottledJd`).

## Published layout variables
CSS custom properties on the document root, so panels never overlap: `--control-panel-bottom` (ControlPanel), `--bottom-bar-height` (BottomBar: distance from the viewport bottom to the bar's top), `--header-bottom` (Header). The drawer's top and bottom, and the phone header and Controls limits, are computed from them (with fallbacks before they are published).

**Breakpoints:** at 1140 px and narrower the control panel collapses behind its Controls button (the 520 px header and the 560 px panel would otherwise meet); at 940 px and narrower the 380 px drawer sits under the header (`top` from `--header-bottom`); at 720 px and narrower the phone layout applies (compact header and bar, the drawer as a bottom sheet). The open Controls dropdown may overlay the header; it is topmost and opaque. Layout tests (`e2e/layout.spec.ts`) check that the header, control panel, bottom bar and drawer (open and closed) do not intersect, and that the page does not scroll horizontally, at 390x844, 800x1000, 1024x768, 1100x800 and 1280x720.

## Look and accessibility
Stable names: the drawer is "Body information"; controls keep their visible names (Orbit, Follow, Readable, True scale, Auto, High, Medium, Low, Camera effects, Now, Go, Tour). Slide and fade transitions are switched off under reduced motion (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` in `app.css`), the drawer scroll to a source uses `auto` instead of `smooth`, and Tour flights become cuts.
